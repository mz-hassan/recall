Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
• Preference elicitation is a generic mechanism for inferring human goals / desires / priorities
• T-REX operated from an imitation learning perspective, but trajectories can come from
anywhere, not just demonstrations

• General recipe for alignment: infer human’s reward function and then optimize it with RL
• …or as we’ll see next time, maybe just learn policies directly from preferences
